# Legal Sources Reference — PT & EU

Where to fetch a citation this skill makes. Pointers only — the verification protocol is Rule 1 in
[`SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md), and the substantive checklists live in `SKILL.md` §6 and
[`checklists/`](./checklists/), so the two don't drift out of sync.

## Official sources

- **Diário da República Eletrónico (DRE)** — https://dre.pt/ — official, consolidated Portuguese
  legislation: Código Civil, Código do Trabalho, and DL/Lei numbers.
- **EUR-Lex** — https://eur-lex.europa.eu/ — official EU regulations and directives, including GDPR,
  in their consolidated versions.
- **PGDLisboa** — http://www.pgdlisboa.pt/ — consolidated, cross-referenced versions of the main
  Portuguese codes; useful for locating a specific article quickly.
- **UNCITRAL** — https://uncitral.un.org/en/texts/salegoods/conventiontexts/sale_goods — authoritative
  text of the CISG. It's a UN convention, not EU law, so it isn't reliably on EUR-Lex — check here
  instead when a contract's object triggers it (Step 3(b)).
- **CNPD** — https://www.cnpd.pt/ — Portugal's data-protection authority. Use for interpretive guidance
  where GDPR/Lei n.º 58/2019's own text doesn't settle a DPA question (breach-notification specifics,
  SCC practicalities, DPIA thresholds) — DRE/EUR-Lex give you the statute, not how it's applied.
