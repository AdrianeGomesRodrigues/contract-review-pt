# MSA Red-Flag Checklist

General tech/B2B-services market practice. Every number below is illustrative (Rule 5).

- **Liability cap** — present and reasonable (roughly 1–2x annual contract value is a common starting point, not a target). Uncapped liability, or carve-outs broad enough to swallow the cap → flag.
- **IP** — deliverables assigned on payment vs. merely licensed; background IP carved out; ownership of AI/automation-assisted outputs addressed where relevant. Where the deliverable is software, ground the flag in the computer-program regime named in Step 3(b). An IP-assignment clause whose scope reaches beyond the stated services object → flag per Rule 4 as a possible mixed/hybrid contract, not just an IP red flag.
- **Payment terms** — NET 30 is typical; late-payment consequences stated.
- **Termination** — for cause vs. for convenience, notice periods, effect on in-flight SOWs.
- **Data protection** — does it reference or attach a DPA where personal data is in scope? Where personal data is processed, absence is a gap; confirm coverage rather than treating it as handled elsewhere.
- **Cross-border hardware deliverables** — assess CISG applicability per Step 3(b) before reaching for an exclusion clause.
- **Individual "contractor" relationships** — hallmarks of subordination (fixed hours, exclusivity, integration into the client's structure) → flag per Step 3(c).
- **Governing law and jurisdiction** — two separate clauses checked against the two instruments in Step 3(d), consistent with the parties' locations.
