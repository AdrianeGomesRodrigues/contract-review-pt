# DPA Red-Flag Checklist

General tech/B2B-services market practice. Every number below is illustrative (Rule 5).

- **Art. 28(3) GDPR required elements** — subject matter, duration, nature/purpose, categories of data and of data subjects all specified.
- **Documented instructions** — processing happens only on them.
- **Confidentiality commitment** of authorised personnel.
- **Security measures** referenced (Art. 32 GDPR).
- **Sub-processor mechanism** — general vs. specific authorisation, with notice and a right to object.
- **Assistance obligations** — data-subject rights (Art. 28(3)(e) GDPR), breach notification (Art. 28(3)(f)), DPIAs (Arts. 35–36 GDPR).
- **Breach-notification timeline** — meaningfully shorter than 72h from processor to controller, so the controller can still meet its own Art. 33 GDPR obligation to the supervisory authority.
- **Deletion or return of data** on termination, with a stated timeline.
- **International-transfer mechanism** named, if applicable (SCCs, adequacy decision).
- **Audit/access rights** present and consistent with Art. 28(3)(h) GDPR. A certification reference (e.g. SOC 2 / ISO 27001) can be supporting evidence of compliance; whether it substitutes for the audit/access right required here goes under Needs Verification, as it is not an automatic equivalent.
